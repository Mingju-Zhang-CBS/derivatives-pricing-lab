# Data and method rights

The MIT license covers newly authored repository code. Public mathematical methods retain their original attribution; QuantLib 1.44 is an independently authored numerical reference under its [BSD-style license](https://www.quantlib.org/license.shtml). No upstream contribution is claimed.

The checked-in parameter grid, simulated paths and quote fixture are generated locally from explicit assumptions. Their results are not observed market prices. The fixture manifest fingerprints omitted row-level outputs that the runnable command recreates locally.

The SPX workflow accepts user-acquired OptionMetrics tables. Institutional access through WRDS does not by itself authorize redistribution of records or derived results. No licensed raw records, per-contract results or real-data aggregate figures are bundled. The real study stays private until applicable vendor/institutional publication rights are established. See [WRDS policy](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/pages/about/data-download-and-analysis-policy/).

Public contract facts and field conventions are attributed in [Quote Research](docs/QUOTE_RESEARCH.md); manuals are linked rather than copied. Original coursework, employer workbooks and client scenarios are excluded.
